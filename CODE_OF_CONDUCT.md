# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Do not:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post other people's private information (this includes real emails from other people: remove personal details from anything you share); or use sexualised language or imagery.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile; reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
